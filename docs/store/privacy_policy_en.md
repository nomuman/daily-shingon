# Privacy Policy (Daily Shingon)

Last updated: 2026-02-28

## 1. Introduction

Daily Shingon (the “App”) is a daily practice companion inspired by Shingon Buddhism.
This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

If you use the App without account sync, no personal information or usage data is sent to external servers.
If you enable sync, your authentication data (such as email) and the data you choose to sync (such as practice logs and settings) are transmitted to Supabase.

## 3. On-Device Storage

Practice logs, settings, and related data are stored locally on your device.
Data is sent to an external server only when you explicitly use account sync.

## 4. Notifications

The App uses notification permission to provide morning/night reminders.
You can turn notifications on/off and adjust times in the App and/or your device settings.

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

When sync is enabled, required data is transmitted to Supabase for synchronization.
We do not share your data for advertising or analytics purposes.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Supabase to provide optional account sync.
We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
If we add another service in the future, we will clearly disclose:

- the service name,
- what data may be processed,
- the purpose,
- and we will update this policy.

## 7. External Links

The App may contain external links (e.g., references and sources).
Information handling on those sites is governed by their own policies.

## 8. Children

The App is not designed specifically for children.
Guardians may use device-level controls as needed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed.
If changes are significant, we will provide notice in the App and/or on the public policy page.
